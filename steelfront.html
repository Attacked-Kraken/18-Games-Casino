❓ How to play

Move with the stick on the left. Look by dragging anywhere on the right side.

Shoot with the big ⌖ button. Keep your finger on it and slide to aim while you fire. Short bursts are more accurate. Head hits do double damage.

Reload, crouch (steadier aim) and jump are on the bottom right. Sniper rifles have a scope button.

Tap a weapon card to switch. Tap the card you're holding to inspect your finish.

✚ Med kit heals. 💣 Frag blasts an area. ❄️ Frost slows everyone it hits for 4 seconds.

Coins come from hits, wins and stars. Spend them in the Armory on new weapons and finishes. Do your daily goals for bonus coins.

Hits make sparks, not blood. Opponents are training-sim soldiers who drop out when tagged.
