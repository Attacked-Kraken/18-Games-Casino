RULES & CONTROLS

DRIVE: left thumbstick to steer · GAS / BRK / REV pedals (or W A S D + arrows on desktop).
DRS: enabled after lap 2 — within 1s of the car ahead in a DRS zone, tap DRS (or press D) for a top-speed boost.
DESKTOP KEYS: D=DRS, P=request pit stop, C=cycle camera, Shift=ERS overtake, arrows/WASD to drive.
ERS: hold ERS for hybrid boost. It recharges under braking.
TIRES: Soft = fast, wears fast · Medium = balanced · Hard = slow, lasts. In a dry race you must use 2 compounds — pit at least once. Inters/Wets for rain.
PIT: tap PIT near the pit entry; pick tires; the crew does the rest.
FLAGS: 3 track-limit warnings = 5s penalty. Blue flags — backmarkers, don't fight them.
WEATHER: it can rain mid-race. Wrong tires in the wet = ice rink.
POINTS: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1, +1 for fastest lap (top 10).
RIVALS: the AI cheats a little — rubber-band pace and hard defending when you're near. That's the game.
