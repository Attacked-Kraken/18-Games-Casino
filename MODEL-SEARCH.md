# Model search (Sketchfab robot)

Add a line and merge - the robot searches Sketchfab for FREE downloadable models (CC Attribution / CC0 only, under
20 MB), best first, 12 per search. The owner looks at them with pictures, spins them in 3D and taps "Get it" on
**tools/model-search.html** (Game Development -> Model Search). The fetch robot then downloads and slims his pick.
Muses: add SEARCH lines for what your game needs, then tell the owner "search ready" - he picks, not you.

Format: `- SEARCH · <words to search> · for <game / what it is>`

- SEARCH · hockey arena · for Ice Hockey venue
- SEARCH · ice rink · for Ice Hockey venue
- SEARCH · baseball stadium · for Baseball venue
- SEARCH · soccer stadium · for Soccer venue
- SEARCH · football stadium · for any big stadium
- SEARCH · race track · for F1 / Exotic Racer / Apex Moto circuits
- SEARCH · formula race car · for F1 cars
- SEARCH · sports car · for Exotic Racer player car + rivals
- SEARCH · supercar · for Exotic Racer player car + rivals
- SEARCH · motorcycle · for Apex Moto bikes
- SEARCH · monster truck · for Monster Truck rivals (his truck stays)
- SEARCH · airplane · for Flight
- SEARCH · running track stadium · for Track & Field venue
- SEARCH · crowd stadium seats · for crowds in every venue
