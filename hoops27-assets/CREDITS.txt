27Hoops real-player stack
-------------------------
Player bodies, faces and hair: Quaternius Universal Base Characters / Superhero
models, CC0 1.0, loaded at runtime from this repo's characters/base-human-v2/
(male.glb + hair). Animation poses come from the game's own rig.
Kit builder (jersey_v7.js), body shader and player builder (hooper_v8.js,
hooper_q.js) and the engine core (gfx.js) are adapted from the repo owner's
player-preview work. GLTFLoader / SkeletonUtils / BufferGeometryUtils are
three.js examples modules (MIT).
