WHAT I SAW: Five car models' wheels do not sit on the road at rest. The
tokii-family models (Kestrel RS, Zenith V12, CRUZ Titan GT) and the V12
Goblin float on ALL FOUR wheels, 0.0745–0.0829 m above the road surface —
the player's own default car, the V12 Goblin, floats. The Futuristic Muscle
floats on its FRONT wheels only (0.0435 m; its rear wheels sit at 0.0064 m).
The only thing near the ground under a floating car is its fake shadow
blob (0.035 m), which is not a wheel. Every other model censused sits
correctly: Patriot models (Vanta GT, Nova Spyder, CRUZ Vortex GT), Phoenix
455, Duke 69 and the SHC models (Brute 69, Phantom X) all have all four
wheels at 0–0.0033 m.
EXPECTED: rules/short/apexracer.md says "wheels on the road — no car ever
missing its wheels"; checklists/apexracer.md R15 says every car model has
wheel meshes and each wheel's lowest point sits on the road surface. The
rule states no numeric tolerance; the R13 band [0, 0.03] m is applied
(gap < 0 = sunk, gap > 0.03 = floating). A wheel at 0.0745 m or 0.0435 m is
not on the road surface.
HOW TO SEE IT:
1. Open apexracer.html, open the GARAGE, and display a tokii model
   (e.g. Kestrel RS) — its four tires hover visibly above the garage floor
   (the floor reference is y = 0: the display root sits at y = 0).
2. Display the V12 Goblin, Zenith V12 or CRUZ Titan GT — same float on all
   four wheels.
3. Display the Futuristic Muscle — its front wheels hover while its rear
   wheels sit on the floor (a nose-up stance).
4. Display a Patriot model (e.g. Vanta GT), the Phoenix 455 or the Duke 69
   — all four wheels sit on the floor (contrast case).
5. Start a New York Circuit 8-lap race with a floating model as the player
   car — at the grid, before driving, the same float is present (cars
   still, resting state). Driving samples are not the gate: wheels dip and
   lift transiently while driving.
6. (Code check) In apexracer.html, makeGlbCar lifts a model by its tyre
   meshes' bounding box (`model.position.y -= tb.min.y`); the tokii/goblin
   tyre bounding boxes are inflated vs their vertices (see MEASURED), so
   the lift under-settles those models.
MEASURED: Vertex-based measurement (actual rendered vertices x matrixWorld;
NOT bounding-box corners — see note below). Units: 1 game unit = 1 m

0.0820 / RRr 0.0801 m; Futuristic Muscle FRONT (LF/RF) 0.0435 / 0.0435 m,
REAR (LRr/RRr) 0.0064 / 0.0064 m. Passing models for contrast:
