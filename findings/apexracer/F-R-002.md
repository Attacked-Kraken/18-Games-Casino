WHAT I SAW: The default player car (V12 Goblin, Class B) is bigger than the REALITY.md supercar table allows, in both the garage and on the grid. Its bounding box measures 5.58 m long and 2.828 m wide, against bands of 3.825–5.52 m long and 1.6575–2.2425 m wide (±15% of the table). Height, 1.5485 m, is inside its 0.935–1.61 m band. The box holds 45 meshes, all in the car-root subtree (body, glass, clearcoat, wheels); one shadow-blob mesh widens the box by 0.106 m, and width still fails without it (2.7219 m). The likely cause is the owner's +20% car scaling (G-01, noted in the game's own G-74 comment: every car wears +20%, so the biggest model measures 5.82 m) — but R12 compares the in-game car against the table, and the in-game car exceeds it.

2. Measure the player car's bounding box (the game's __reality.player root, local axes where +Z is the nose): 5.58 m long, 2.828 m wide, 1.5485 m high.

4. Compare with the ±15% bands: length band tops at 5.52 m, width band tops at 2.2425 m — both exceeded; height band 0.935–1.61 m — met.
5. (Code check) Search apexracer.html for the G-74 comment: every car wears the owner's +20% (G-01).
