# Phone checklist (owner, about 5 min per game, on his iPhone)

The robots check every game every night. This list covers what only a real phone in a real hand can show.
A game goes to the Apex Arcade only after it has a dated ✅ row here (owner's call, as always).

1. Opens in under 5 seconds.
2. Sound plays after the first tap.
3. Hold the stick, pull down a notification or switch apps, then come back: nothing keeps moving by itself.
4. Nothing sits under the notch or the home bar.
5. Win, lose and Play again all work.
6. Play 3 rounds in a row: no lag, no freeze.
7. Add to Home Screen, turn on airplane mode, open it: it still opens (only once offline play is built, see STORE-READY.md).
