HOW TO PLAY

Right stick — push up to drive, pull down to brake or reverse.

Left stick — steer. Monster trucks have 4-wheel steering, so it turns tight.

BOOST — hold the boost button for a burst of speed.

In the air — push the right stick up or down to pitch the truck for flips.

Crush cars — drive over the car lines to flatten them for points. Big jumps and long air score bonus points, and the crowd goes wild.

Rally career — finish each event to unlock the next one.

Play a friend — share your code from the menu and race your friend's best score.
