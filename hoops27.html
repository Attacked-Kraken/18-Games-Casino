How to play

Offense

  1. Stick (left) moves your player. Hold ⚡ Sprint to go faster.
  2. 🏀 Shoot: hold it to rise up, let go when the meter hits the green. Bright green = perfect. Near the hoop the same button does a layup or a dunk.
  3. 👐 Pass: passes to the open teammate, or the one your stick points at. You always control the player with the ball.
  4. 🌀 Move: a crossover. Good ball-handlers can drop a defender.

Defense

  1. Stay between your man and the hoop. Your player has the gold ring.
  2. ✋ Block jumps to contest or block a shot. 🫳 Steal reaches for the ball (too many reaches = fouls).
  3. 🔄 Switch takes control of the defender closest to the ball.

The rules

  1. 4 quarters, 24-second shot clock, threes beyond the arc, fouls on shots give free throws, and-ones count.
  2. Leave a game any time: it saves exactly where you were and Continue game brings you right back. Restart never counts toward your season record.
