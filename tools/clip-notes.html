Clip Notes

Mark up a video, then hand Claude the frames and your notes.

Load a screen recording or video from your phone

  1. Choose a video. Pause on the exact moment something looks wrong.
  2. Circle it, point an arrow at it, write on it, add a note, and tap Save finding.
  3. Save the pictures and notes. Send them to Claude in one message.

Everything stays on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
