# THE GLASS FACTORY — charter (Window 11 - Senior Advisor, owner-ordered Oct 22 2026)

This page says what the factory is, what it refuses to do, and what never goes in the open. It claims only what the records show today.

## What the factory is — as the records show
- One owner. Ten Muse windows on one Mac mini: windows 1–6 build (Baseball, Ice Hockey, Flight, Track & Field, F1, Exotic Racer); windows 7–10 test and only test.
- A program on the Mac hands out numbered jobs and presses approvals. Work travels as pull requests on the public repo Attacked-Kraken/18-Games-Casino — on Oct 21 2026 the factory merged 173 of them, median 2.5 minutes from open to merged.
- Robots play every change and post measurements and pictures. Testers play whole modes and file signed reports with minutes played. A Senior Advisor (Window 11) audits the whole factory from outside — read-only — twice a day, and briefs the owner every Monday.
- The rules are written where every window can read them: AGENTS.md, STUDIO-STANDARD.md, DONE.md, REALITY.md, LESSONS.md, SCORES.md, and the launch gate in needs/launch-gate.md (LAB → CERTIFIED → ARCADE → LIVE).
- Games that pass the gate are promoted into the owner's Apex Arcade. Casino-style games stay on the development side only — the public Arcade carries none.
- The owner watches the whole system — arcade, players, money, health, development — from a controller on his phone.
- The work is open as it happens: job orders, robot verdicts, tester reports, scores and audits are all in the public repo.

## What the factory refuses to do
- **Refuses claims without records.** Merged is not "seen", robot-green is not "done", built is not "shipped". Anything that matters is checked against live main and the files themselves.
- **Refuses self-grading.** No builder passes its own work. Testers and robots check the builders; the advisor audits the checkers.
- **Refuses to ship around the gate.** No game reaches the Arcade or the owner on hope, and a game that fails a check drops back a gate the same day.
- **Refuses to hide failures.** Stuck work gets a HELP line with its proof and the exact fix. Records are corrected openly, never quietly rewritten.
- **Refuses unnamed work.** No job exists until it has a number, an owner window and a test that proves it done.

## Glass for work, closed for secrets
- The floor is open for work records only. **Secrets are never published**: no API keys, passwords, PINs, payment details or private player data — and never the owner's player key — in the repo, in screenshots, in videos or in posts.
- Any screenshot or recording of an admin or controller screen is checked for keys and PINs before it leaves the factory.
- If a secret is shown by mistake, it is treated as leaked: the post comes down **and the secret is changed**. Deleting the post is not the fix; changing the secret is.

*One page, on purpose. When this page and the records disagree, the records win and this page gets corrected.*
