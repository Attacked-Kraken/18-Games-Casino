# Rules: track & field (apextrackfield.html)

## Must enforce
- **Starts:** "On your marks", "Set", then the gun. Moving before the gun is a **false start** (the runner is
  disqualified). Sprinters start from **blocks**.
- **Lanes:** the 100 m, 200 m and 400 m are run in lanes for the whole race. Stepping on or over the lane line on a
  bend is a disqualification. The 800 m breaks from lanes after the first bend. Longer races are run on the inside
  of lane 1.
- **Staggered starts** so every lane runs the same distance.
- **Finish:** placed by the **torso** crossing the line. Photo finish to 1/100 s. Times shown to 0.01 s.
- **Hurdles:** knocking hurdles down is allowed, but going around one is a disqualification.
- **Relays:** baton passed inside the 30 m changeover zone, or the team is disqualified.
- **Jumps:** a foul if the take-off foot passes the board (long / triple). High jump: 3 tries per height.
- **Throws:** the athlete must stay in the circle or behind the arc until the throw lands, and it must land inside
  the sector. 3 attempts (plus 3 more for the top 8).
- **Wind:** a tailwind over +2.0 m/s means the time doesn't count as a record (shown as "w").

## Game flow
- Heats, then a semi-final, then the final. Results table after each event.
- Medals: gold, silver, bronze, and a podium.

## Broadcast look
- Lane graphics with names and countries (our own made-up ones) before the start, and a running clock.
- A tracking camera along the straight. Replay of the finish (photo-finish picture).

## Robot checks
- A start before the gun is a false start; a runner leaving the lane on a bend is disqualified
- Long jump foul when the foot passes the board; the finish order is by torso
