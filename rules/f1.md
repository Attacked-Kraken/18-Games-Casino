# Rules: F1-style racing (apexf1.html)

## Must enforce
- **Qualifying** sets the grid (fastest lap first). The grid is 2 by 2, staggered.
- **Start:** 5 red lights come on one by one, then all go out. GO. Moving before the lights go out = jump start
  (a 5-second penalty).
- **Track limits:** all 4 wheels over the white line and gaining time = the lap time is deleted (in qualifying), or a
  warning then a 5-second penalty (in the race).
- **Pit stops:** the pit lane speed limit (80 km/h), tyre change. A race must use 2 different tyre compounds (soft /
  medium / hard: grip versus wear).
- **Flags:**
  - **yellow:** slow down, no overtaking in that sector
  - **blue:** let the faster car through
  - **red:** race stopped
  - **chequered:** finish
- **Safety car:** after a crash, no overtaking. The field bunches up, then restarts.
- **Collisions** causing a crash: a time penalty for the car at fault.
- **Points** for the top 10: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.

## Game flow
- Practice (optional), then qualifying, then the race (short or full laps). The championship table between races.

## Broadcast look
- Timing tower on the left (positions and gaps), lap counter, speed, gear and RPM, tyre icon.
- Onboard camera, chase camera and TV corner camera. Replays of overtakes.

## Robot checks
- Lights-out start sequence of 5; a jump start gives a penalty; a pit stop keeps to the pit speed limit
- Track limits are counted; the points table is correct for the top 10
