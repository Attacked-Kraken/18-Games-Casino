# Feel targets - STARTING POINTS (filed Oct 25 2026 from an outside AI answer; NOT yet verified against real games)

Status: DRAFT. These are generic rules of thumb. A builder may change a number only with a reason + a picture/clip. The robot measures what it can; the owner's 1-5 feel tap is the real judge. Each game's looks/<game>.md REFERENCES + MUST MATCH lines override these for that game.

## First 60 seconds (measurable)
- Play tap to first controllable action: 3 s or less.
- First scored / successful event: 45 s of game time or less.
- At least one sound + one visual juice event in the first 20 s of play.
- At most one short on-screen tip, gone after the first successful action.

## Motion weight
- Runners / skaters: 70-80 % of top speed in 0.35-0.55 s; stopping is stronger than starting.
- Contact frames (foot plant, stick contact, bat/ball, wheel down) slightly emphasised; 2-4 frame settle or squash on landings.
- Camera follow lag 0.08-0.18 s on the main axis; more on big impacts.
- Hit-stop 40-90 ms on solid contacts (shot, check, landing).
- Input buffer 80-120 ms.

## Camera starting points
- Hockey: slightly raised, behind/above the puck carrier, moderate lag, boards feel close; small punch-in on big hits.
- Baseball: tight view for the pitch, wider once the ball is in play; never lose the ball for more than a fraction of a second.
- Track & Field: side / three-quarter, athlete large, low height so hurdles read, short lag.
- Flight / F1: chase camera, controlled lag, height + distance change with speed, short push/shake on overtakes, near misses, landings.
- One short camera spec per game (distance, height, lag, big-event behaviour); a deviation is a defect.

## Juice, cheapest first
1 hit-stop 40-90 ms; 2 screen shake scaled to event size; 3 sound on every core action + crowd swell; 4 particle burst / flash on impact; 5 small camera punch-in or FOV change on big successes; 6 squash / settle on landings; 7 phone haptic if available; 8 short slow-motion / replay sting on goals and finishes; 9 floating score numbers; 10 crowd audio + simple crowd reaction.

## First-5-minutes harsh flags (the art director / a review Muse writes one ranked page per game in this tone)
floaty or unresponsive first touch; no idea what to do (tip missing or too long); camera loses the action or clips; big actions silent or weightless; buttons hard to hit or under the notch / home bar; blurry look or wrong athletes (proportions, gear, floating feet); nothing rewarding in the first minute; sound does not start or mute state confusing; stutter on a mid-range phone; cannot tell if winning.

## Weekly then-vs-now (idea, NOT built; owner decides)
Same game state + same camera, robot picture and clip from 3 weeks ago beside today's; the owner (or one outside eye) gives ONE 1-5 "better than before" score on feel, clarity and weight - not rule-following. Priority games weekly.
