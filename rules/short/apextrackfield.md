# Track & Field - the short list (apextrackfield.html, builder window 4, tester window 10)
1. KEEP (locked, J7): the stadium + crowd, the true 400 m 8-lane track, the studio runners with real run cycles, the sharp picture, the start camera (7 m back, 2.8 m up, 10 m ahead).
2. Every event is the REAL sport start to finish: rivals jump the same hurdles (drawn in their lanes), relay hands a real baton, pole vault has a pole and clears a bar, high jump goes over the bar, throws fly and land with a marker.
3. Everyone starts ON the start line together; the 400 m and long races are winnable (rivals pay the same stamina); no race is unwinnable by design.
4. Hurdle heights real: 100 m 0.838 m, 110 m 1.067 m, 300/400 m 0.914 m; feet on the track (REALITY contact at the toe, gait()).
5. No box/banner ever covers the runner or the HUD; menus fit a 390 px tall sideways phone on any fonts.
6. Career: every level loads, plays, unlocks the next; nothing blocks TAP RUN; two thumbs at once always work; dev Skip only with dev=1.
7. Discus/shot/javelin: ONE action (hold to spin, release), camera follows the implement; long races: low chase camera around the track.
8. Controls come from kit/controls.js only; every hold releases on blur/call/app switch.
9. Play tests use Playwright calls (page.evaluate, page.touchscreen) - never Puppeteer ones; measurements in game time.
10. One fix per job; a play test proves every owner point; report POINT k: YES/NO.
