# Baseball - the short list (apexbaseball.html, builder window 1, tester window 7)
1. KEEP (locked): the batting flow aim → windup → swing → flight → fielding, three outfielders who catch, day light, the camera options, the crowd that reacts, the music + mute.
2. A real ballpark: the owner's Night-Ballpark kit loads with its crowd (kit/stadiums/Night-Ballpark/) - never the bare field.
3. Real rigged players (characters/base-human-v2 + baseball kit), true size 1.75-1.95 m, feet on the ground (REALITY hook with go/time/speed/camera).
4. Sharp: pixel ratio min(devicePixelRatio, 1.5), never lowered for speed.
5. Baseball by its rules: pitch count moves, 3 strikes = out, 4 balls = walk, count resets each batter, 3 outs end the half, runs score - each a passing play test.
6. A close camera that frames the batter (the robot's close-up must show a face, not a helmet over it).
7. No green tint, no night-dark field, no real brands/teams/players, nothing built from boxes.
8. Controls come from kit/controls.js only (no own stick code).
9. One fix per job; a play test proves every owner point; report POINT k: YES/NO.
