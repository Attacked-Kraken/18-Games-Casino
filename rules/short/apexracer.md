# Exotic Racer - the short list (apexracer.html, builder window 6)
1. Fetched cars show their own paint (body material by name, never one turn copied to another model), right size, nose forward, wheels on the road - no car ever missing its wheels.
2. The race world loads in slices with a live loading screen - never one frozen synchronous build.
3. Garage picture (games.json "pictures": garage) shows color, size and facing at a glance.
4. REALITY hook kind car: right way round the track, go(), time(), speed(), camera.
5. Sharp picture, phone budget, no real brands or badges on any car.
6. Controls come from kit/controls.js only; let go = stop.
7. Change only the piece the job names - never rewrite placement, steering, AI or cameras on the way.
8. One fix per job; a play test proves every owner point; report POINT k: YES/NO.
