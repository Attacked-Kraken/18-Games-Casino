APEX CAR ENGINE SOUNDS
======================
63 MP3 files for 3 original vehicles (21 sounds per vehicle). They are mono 44.1 kHz and loudness-matched.
  grand_prix/     open-wheel race car (high-revving V6 sound with a turbo whine)
  supercar/       exotic road car (smooth V12)
  monster_truck/  big V8 with a blower whine (matches the existing monster truck pack)
Each folder contains:
  Loops (2.00 s, seamless): idle_loop, accel_low_loop, accel_mid_loop, accel_high_loop,
    decel_loop, cruise_loop, tire_squeal_loop
  One-shots: rev_short, rev_long, rev_blips, shift_up, shift_down, start_up, shut_down,
    accel_sweep, decel_sweep, backfire_1-3, tire_squeal_short, tire_squeal_long
manifest.json lists every file with its loop flag, length and rpm.
USING THE LOOPS AS AN ENGINE: play all four loops (idle, accel_low, accel_mid, accel_high) at once.
Crossfade their volumes by throttle, and raise playbackRate by about 0.9-1.25 as speed rises.
preview.html shows a working example: open it, press Start engine and drag the throttle.
Safari note: MP3 adds a small silence at the start of a file. For gapless loops, use WebAudio
(decodeAudioData plus loop=true, as the preview does) rather than <audio loop>.
All content is original and generated by code. There are no brands, logos, real teams or recorded voices.
