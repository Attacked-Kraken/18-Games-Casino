APEX STADIUM SOUNDS
===================
109 MP3 files in 6 sport folders: soccer, football, hockey, baseball, basketball, track.
Every sport has: ambience_loop, ambience_quiet_loop, cheer_1, cheer_2, big_cheer, groan,
gasp_ooh, applause, chant_1, chant_2, clap_rhythm.
The crowds and chants are wordless and synthesized, so no real voices are used.
Sport extras:
  soccer      whistles, kick, ball_net
  football    whistles, tackle_hit, ball_kick, stadium_horn, drum_line_sting
  hockey      whistles, goal horns, period buzzer, puck slapshot/pass/boards/post, skate_stop, organ_sting
  baseball    bat_crack, foul_tip, glove_catch, organ stings
  basketball  whistles, buzzers, dribble, net_swish, sneaker_squeak, rim_clank
  track       starting_gun, timer_beep, start_beeps, runner_footsteps, whistle
Files ending in _loop loop seamlessly. manifest.json lists every file.
Open preview.html to listen. It is grouped by sport, and purple buttons are loops.
All content is original and generated by code. There are no brands, logos, real teams or recorded voices.
