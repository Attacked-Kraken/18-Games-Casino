APEX WEATHER + RACING FX SOUNDS
===============================
24 MP3 files. They are the sound companion for the Weather-Racing-FX kit and also work on their own.
Weather: rain_light, rain_medium, rain_heavy, rain_on_car_roof (loops), wind_loop, wind_strong_loop,
  wind_gust, snow_ambience, fog_ambience (loops), thunder_crack, thunder_rumble_far, thunder_close,
  lightning_zap
Racing: tire_smoke_hiss_loop, skid_short, skid_long, dirt_spray, gravel_spray, dirt_spray_loop,
  sparks_scrape, metal_scrape_long, puddle_splash, puddle_splash_big, wet_tires_loop
manifest.json gives each file's kind (weather or racing) and loop flag. Open preview.html to listen.
To use these sounds inside the Weather-Racing-FX preview, unzip both kits into the same parent folder.
All content is original and generated by code. There are no brands, logos, real teams or recorded voices.
