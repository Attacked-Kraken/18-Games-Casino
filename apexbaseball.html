APEX BASEBALL

APEX ARCADE · 3 INNINGS

You are the home Comets (blue) hosting the Sharks (teal). You play BOTH sides: you BAT in the bottom of each inning and you PITCH in the top of each inning, against the CPU.

  • PITCHING: pick FASTBALL / CURVEBALL / CHANGEUP (123), tap the zone pad to aim, press THROW, then tap LOCK when the needle is in the green (or do nothing — it throws itself after a moment). Miss the green and the pitch sails.
  • BATTING: watch the CPU windup, press SWING (space) as the ball reaches the glowing ring. Don't swing at bad pitches — 4 balls is a walk, 3 strikes and you're out. Fouls count as strikes (but can't strike you out).
  • Fielding and base running are automatic, with real force-out and double-play rules. Extra innings if tied after 3. Walk-off wins end it on the spot.

Tip: fastballs are straight and quick · curveballs break late · changeups float and drop. Early swing = pull, late = opposite field.
