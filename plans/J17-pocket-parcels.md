# PLAN · JOB J17 · Pocket Parcels   (one page - the owner reads it on his phone)

**The 30-second fun:** Parcels slide down a conveyor toward you; you tap-and-drag each one to the mailbox of the same color before the timer runs out. Fast, satisfying, streaks build.

**The first 60 seconds:** One parcel slides down, a big arrow points to the matching mailbox, you drag it there — DING, +100, confetti. Second parcel comes faster. By 60s you're juggling 3 colors and chasing a streak.

**Controls:** left thumb = drag parcels · right thumb = (free, game is one-thumb) · buttons on screen: big PAUSE top-right, streak meter top-center. Tap a parcel to grab, drag to the mailbox, release to drop.

**Camera (ONE spec):** Fixed top-down tilted 30°, conveyor runs bottom-to-top of screen, 4 mailboxes across the top (red/blue/green/yellow), never moves.

**Looks like (3 reference pictures of real games):**
1. Diner Dash-style conveyor sorting — copy the clear color-matching and urgency
2. Fruit Ninja — copy the satisfying tap-drag-release feel and juice (confetti, pop sounds)
3. Papers Please parcel desk — copy the tactile parcel look (brown boxes, tape, labels)
