APEX GRAND PRIX TRACK SOUNDS
============================
22 MP3 files for the Grand-Prix-Track event layer. Unzip this folder next to the Grand-Prix-Track folder.
All sounds are wordless with no voices. Files ending in _loop are seamless.

Reused from Stadium-Sounds (soccer crowd, copied and renamed):
  crowd_ambience_loop, crowd_ambience_quiet_loop, crowd_cheer_1, crowd_cheer_2, crowd_big_cheer,
  crowd_applause, crowd_gasp
New (synthesized):
  crowd_roar_loop (positional; volume follows how close the cars are), crowd_roar_pass (swell as a car
  passes), start_light_beep, lights_out_go, finish_horn, fireworks_launch, fireworks_bang_1,
  fireworks_bang_2, fireworks_crackle, confetti_pop, helicopter_loop, flag_flutter_loop, pa_chime,
  podium_fanfare, car_pass_whoosh
manifest.json lists every file with its loop flag and source. Open preview.html to listen.
grand-prix-event.js loads these from soundBase (default '../Grand-Prix-Track-Sounds/').
