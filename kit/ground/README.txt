APEX GROUND TEXTURES
====================
24 seamless (tileable) ground textures:
asphalt_plain, asphalt_worn, asphalt_wet, kerb_red_white, kerb_blue_white, kerb_green_white,
grass_plain, pitch_stripes, pitch_checker, grass_rough, dirt, dirt_tracks, gravel, ice_rink,
baseball_infield, baseball_dirt_dragged, running_track, running_track_plain, sand, concrete,
concrete_plain, snow, mud, cobblestone
  1024/        1024x1024 colour PNGs
  512/         512x512 colour PNGs (lighter, good for phones)
  normal_512/  512x512 normal maps for lit 3D or 2D scenes (OpenGL style, green = up)
The colour PNGs are reduced to 256 colours to keep the download small.
Use them as a repeating background (CSS background-repeat, canvas createPattern, or a
three.js RepeatWrapping texture).
Open preview.html to see each texture tiled. It has 1024, 512 and Normal toggles and a scale slider.
This kit is split into Ground-Textures-part1.zip and part2.zip. Unzip both into the same place
and they merge into a single Ground-Textures folder.
All content is original and generated by code. There are no brands, logos, real teams or recorded voices.
