APEX BACKGROUNDS PACK
=====================
45 original phone backgrounds, generated from scratch. Free to use in your paid apps.

15 THEMES x 3 VARIANTS
  city_day (morning, sunset, teal)       city_night (blue, purple, amber)     neon_city (magenta, cyan, orange)
  space (violet, teal, crimson)          ocean (day, sunset, night)           underwater (reef, lagoon, deep)
  forest (day, sunset, night)            desert (day, sunset, night)          snow (day, sunset, night)
  volcano (red, dark, orange)            sky / floating islands (day, sunset, night)
  cave (crystal, moss, amethyst)         candy (pink, grape, mint)            stadium (night, day, sunset)
  racetrack (day, sunset, night)

FILES (folder per theme)
  <theme>/<name>_portrait.png     1170 x 2532 (iPhone portrait), full picture
  <theme>/<name>_landscape.png    2532 x 1170 (landscape), the same scene laid out wide
  <theme>/layers/<name>_portrait_far.png / _mid.png / _near.png
      parallax layers for every portrait background. far is solid; mid and near are transparent PNGs.
      Stack far, then mid, then near. Move them at different speeds (e.g. far 0.1x, mid 0.4x, near 1x).
  thumbs/       small JPG previews     manifest.json  list of every file and its layers
  preview.html  gallery: tap any background for full screen; layered ones show live parallax.

DOWNLOAD PARTS
  The pack is split into Backgrounds-Pack-part1.zip and Backgrounds-Pack-part2.zip (each under 22 MB).
  Unzip all parts into the same place. They merge into a single Backgrounds-Pack folder.
  preview.html, README and thumbs are in part 1.

No text, logos or brands appear in any image.
