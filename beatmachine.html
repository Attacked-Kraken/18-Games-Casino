Each block plays one bar of a pattern. Tap a block to change its pattern (A→B→C→D). Build adds a riser and noise sweep, Drop hits an impact. Turn on SONG MODE to play and export the whole arrangement.

CHORDS

Everything is locked to the key, so it always sounds right. Tap the piano roll to draw a melody, or turn on REC KEYS and play the keyboard while the beat runs. Tap a chord to hear it (and record it when REC is on). A progression can fill this bar (one chord per beat) or spread across patterns A–D as a 4-bar song. Tap a chord slot to clear it.

LOAD YOUR SOUND

Tap LOAD SOUND FILE and pick an MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG or AIFF from Files. On a computer you can also drag a file anywhere onto this page. Apple Music songs are copy-protected and can't be loaded.

Works on the sound selected in the library below (your files, mic sounds and 🎤 vocal takes). Each effect makes a new sound, so the original stays safe. The graph shows detected pitch: grey is before, cyan is after.
