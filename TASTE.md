# TASTE.md — what Mr. Cruz likes, in his own words (read with LESSONS.md before EVERY job)

Owner: Mr. Cruz. Plays on his iPhone, sideways. He judges only what he can SEE and PLAY. This file is the factory's
design sense: it grows from his 👍 / 👎 and notes. Add ONE line at the bottom when he gives a new one:
`- YYYY-MM-DD · game · what he said → the design rule`. Never delete a line. Quote him, don't reword him.

## The big picture
- Real sports, played by the real rules. Opponents follow the same rules as the player. If it isn't how the real sport works, it's wrong.
- "Sharp." Crisp pictures on his phone beat extra effects. Never blur or auto-lower quality.
- Finish what is there before adding anything. "Tight" beats "big".
- He wants to PLAY the best version, not read about it. Show a Play button, pictures, a short list of what changed.

## Look and feel
- House look = F1 / Monster Truck menus: clean, big, readable. No green film, no green typewriter lettering, no monospace menus.
- Real 3D models for anything the player drives or controls (not boxes and cylinders). Real sizes, real stadiums with stands all around and a crowd.
- Venues: sloped real ramps, real stands. Box stands, stripes and box ramps were called "horrendous".
- Camera: ONE clear spec (distance, height, look point, follows the player), never through walls, never behind the start line.
- Nothing ever covers the player or the score. No box or banner over the thrower, the car or the HUD.

## Controls (phone, sideways)
- Every button fully on screen and big enough for a thumb (44 px or more). A start button below the screen is a failure.
- Two thumbs at once must work. Holding the stick must never block the action buttons.
- Menus must be usable at 390 px tall (a real sideways phone).

## What "done" means to him
- When he approves something it must be DONE: no false talk, no guessing. Say "NOT CHECKED" until the robot or a picture proves it.
- Every point he names becomes one fix and one play test that proves it. Do ONLY what he said; keep everything else exactly as it is.
- Faster means faster ("boost is useless, I just want it faster"). Bigger means bigger. Do not invent extras.

## How to talk to him
- Plain words, short. No pasted code. Do the thing for him; never send him to run commands.
