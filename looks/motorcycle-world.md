# Look board — motorcycle-world (JOB J2)

Owner's look direction for the motorcycle models. The NEVER list is law:
if a model breaks a NEVER rule, it is NOT DONE.

## Style
- Realistic sport motorcycles (ASSET-LAW: our own original design, CC0 only)
- Clean paint with metalflake, chrome exhaust/forks, dark tires with visible tread
- Rider in racing leathers, full-face helmet, leaning forward

## NEVER list
- NEVER: wheels as hollow see-through tori or sinking below the ground — every wheel needs a solid rim/disc filling the center and must rest on y=0 (nothing below the ground plane).
