# Reference pictures — Apex F1 (`apexf1.html`)

Owner (2026-10-18): Muses guess the look when a game has no target picture. This is the visual target for Apex F1.

## The look
A realistic Formula 1 racer for phones: sleek open-wheel single-seaters with proper F1 silhouettes (front wing, halo, rear wing, slick tires), racing on a proper circuit with grandstands, pit buildings, and track barriers. Think F1 Mobile Racing / F1 25 on console, scaled to run on a phone.

## Reference games (what good looks like)
- **F1 Mobile Racing** (official FIA game): real cars on championship circuits, clean mobile-friendly graphics — https://en.uptodown.com/android/f1
- **F1 25** (console): cockpit view with dust smears, shifting lights, detailed cars — https://kotaku.com/games/f1-25
- **F1 Clash** (mobile): recognizable F1 silhouettes, clean readability — https://appadvice.com/app/f1-clash-official-f1-game/1434561536

## Visual targets
- **Cars:** Open-wheel F1 silhouette — low nose, front wing, halo over cockpit, big rear wing, wide slick tires. Custom Apex livery (no real team brands). Glossy paint, visible tire detail.
- **Track:** Asphalt circuit with red/white curbs, grass runoff, catch fencing, grandstands with crowd, pit building, start/finish gantry. Day race, clear sky.
- **Camera:** Chase cam behind and above the car (like F1 Mobile Racing), showing the track ahead and nearby rivals.
- **HUD:** Minimal — position (P1/20), lap counter, speed. Clean screen rule: no walls of text.
- **Lighting:** Bright daylight, `field` preset. Sharp shadows under cars.

## NEVER
- No real F1 team liveries (Ferrari red, Mercedes silver, Red Bull navy/yellow), no driver names, no "Formula 1" branding — original Apex designs only.
- No boxy/cartoon cars — real F1 proportions.
- No empty gray track with no scenery — grandstands, barriers, and trackside detail are required.
